# Paw Mall — User Guide

How the app works for pet owners. The app is in Spanish by default and in English with the globe
switch in the search box or in Ajustes; this guide uses the English names, with the Spanish ones
in parentheses the first time.

## Tabs

| Tab                         | For                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Explore** (Explorar)      | Pet businesses: vets, vet hospitals, pet stores, boutiques, groomers, boarding, feed stores, pet funerals, trainers |
| **Pet-friendly**            | Places you can go with your pet: restaurants, cafés, lodging, museums, parks                                        |
| **Emergencies** (Urgencias) | The nearest vet hospitals and 24-hour vets, open ones first                                                         |
| **Favorites** (Favoritos)   | Places you saved, newest first                                                                                      |
| **Settings** (Ajustes)      | Language, units, your pet, which places to show                                                                     |

The ⓘ at the top right of a tab explains it.

## Explore and Pet-friendly

The two tabs are one page with the same map and controls; only the places (and their category
chips) change. Whatever you do on one — move the map, zoom, rotate it, switch to satellite,
search — the other shows too.

### Map and list

- **Map / List** switch the view; both count the same places.
- The **green circle** is the search radius around you (or around a place you searched). The
  count at the bottom says how many places are inside it; tap it to see the whole circle.
- With more places in view than the **pin button** shows (200 at first), the map groups them into
  **clusters** (turquoise bubbles with a count). Tap one to zoom to it: the circle moves to where
  those places are. The pin button, above the layers button, steps through 50, 100, 150, 200 and
  300; the choice is kept.
- With nothing in the circle, the count and the empty message still say where: "No places within
  5 km of Chiautempan" (the nearest municipality, or the place you searched).
- **Pins** have their category's color and icon; tap a pin, then its bubble, to open the place.
- **Satellite** (layers button) and the **compass** (turns the map north up) are at the bottom
  right.

### My location (Mi ubicación)

The location button (crosshair) brings the map back to you, zoomed to the smallest radius (5 km,
or 3 mi in miles). It's **on** (filled) while the search is around you.

It turns **off** when you move or zoom the map by hand, tap a cluster, pick a place from the
suggestions, or press 🔍 on a place's name (as soon as the question comes up; it stays off for
"As a place" and Cancel). The button is a switch: tap it while on to turn it off (the search and the map stay as they are).
It turns **on** with the button, when you answer "As a business
name", or when the search box is emptied (✕, or deleting every character; the map goes back
around you, the radius stays). Nothing else changes it.

### Radius

The radius button (e.g. **5 km**) steps through 5, 10, 15, 25, 50, 80 and 160 km (3 to 100 miles
in miles). Zooming the map by hand also sets the radius to what you zoomed to.

### Category chips

**All** (Todos) or one category at a time: Vets, Hospitals, Stores, Boutiques… on Explore;
Restaurants, Cafés, Lodging, Museums, Parks on Pet-friendly.

### Search box

- **Type a business name** ("Huellitas", "estética canina"): the list and map show businesses
  with those words, around you. Word order doesn't matter, and accents and capitals don't either.
  Short words (up to 3 letters) must start a word of the name: "vet me" finds "Vet Me".
- **Type a kind of business** ("veterinaria", "pensión para perros", "croquetas", "hotel para
  perros", "dog grooming"; on Pet-friendly "café", "restaurante", "hotel", "parque"): you get
  every place of that kind, whatever its name. One typo in a long word is fine ("veterinria"),
  and words about the pet ("perros", "mascotas", "dog") are then ignored.
- **Suggestions** drop down as you type (from 3 letters): places (cities, colonias, landmarks)
  first, then businesses nearby. Every word you type must start a word of the suggestion, in any
  order: "puerto vall", "vallarta puerto" and "v puerto" all suggest Puerto Vallarta.
  "CDMX" and "DF" mean Ciudad de México; small words like "en", "near" or "para" are ignored.
  - Tap a **place** to search around it (the circle moves there). Moving the map by hand
    afterwards lets go of the place: the search box empties and the search follows the map, with
    the place's radius ("Mi ubicación" stays off). Zooming keeps the place and changes its radius.
  - Tap a **business** to open its page.
- **A place's name with the search key** (e.g. type "Guadalajara" and press 🔍): the app asks
  whether you mean the place or businesses with that name:
  - **As a place** — search around Guadalajara (moving the map lets go of it, as above).
  - **As a business name** — businesses called "Guadalajara" near you.
  - **Cancel** — keep editing.
- **A place with other words and the search key** ("veterinaria Tlaxcala", "Tlaxcala
  veterinaria", "veterinaria en Mascota", "puerto vallarta taco"): the search goes to the place
  and looks for those words or that kind of business there. When it could also be a business's
  name (a business near you has all those words in its name, or the place's name is a word like
  "Mascota"), the app asks first: **In Mascota** or **By that name** (every word, in names, near
  you).
- **Other text with the search key** (no place in it, e.g. "forrajeria"): searched as
  business names, with no question; it counts as confirmed for the two points below.
- **The radius fits a confirmed search** (a place picked from the suggestions, the question
  answered, or other text sent with the search key): it closes in to the smallest preset that still holds every match, or, when nothing
  matches inside it, opens out to the smallest preset that reaches the nearest match (e.g.
  "Guadalupe" as a business name: nothing within 5 km, so 10 km). Once per answer, on the tab
  that shows it; switching tabs keeps the same circle. The radius button still changes it after.
- **The tab that finds more takes the focus**: after a confirmed search, if the other tab
  (Explorar or Pet-friendly) finds more places inside the circle, or, with none inside either, a
  nearer one, the app switches to it (e.g. "Toluca" on Explorar finds only a café, so it opens
  Pet-friendly).
- **✕** clears the search and brings the full list back.
- **ES / EN** (globe) switches the app's language.

### Pet-friendly specifics

- By default only places **confirmed** to take pets show, filtered by **your pet** (Settings →
  My pet: dog or cat, and your dog's size).
- When nothing confirmed matches a search, it **widens** to venues nobody has answered for yet,
  with a notice.
- A place's **pet policy** says which pets, sizes, where (terrace, indoors) and any fee, with its
  source and date.

## A place's page

- **Map** with the place, its **name**, **category** and a **heart** to save it to Favorites.
- **Address** — tap it to copy the name and address; the share button sends them to any app.
- **How far it's confirmed**, under the address: verified by the owner, confirmed by Paw Mall users,
  confirmed by its own website, or only from a public directory (with the date it was last
  checked); places users reported show "under review".
- **Directions** (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), **Call**, **Website**, **Social media**.
- **Hours**: confirmed hours with "open now", or "Hours not confirmed" with **See hours**, which
  asks Google once (a small free allowance; the answer is shown for this visit only).
- **Pet policy** (venues): confirm it or say it's wrong; for a venue without one, answer
  **"Does it take dogs?"**.
- **Do you own this business?** — claim it to verify and update it.
- **Has this place closed?** and **Report animal cruelty** — one report per phone; the place is
  hidden until reviewed. Reports keep nothing about you or your phone.
- The **sources** of the information are at the bottom.

## Emergencies

The nearest vet hospitals and 24-hour vets within your radius (or around a place you searched),
the first one highlighted. The count at the top names the same circle as the map ("29 places
within 5 km of Cuauhtémoc"); with none inside, it says where. "Open now" refreshes every minute. Small icons show which have a
phone, website or social page; open a card for directions and call. Cards whose details the owner
hasn't verified say to call before going, and reported ones say they're under review.

## Favorites

Places saved with the heart, newest first. They're stored on your phone only.

## Settings

- **Language** — Spanish or English.
- **Distance units** — kilometers or miles.
- **My pet** — dog or cat and your dog's size, for Pet-friendly; **Only confirmed places** (off
  also shows venues nobody has answered for yet, never those that said no).
- **Places** — only places with a phone, with a website, with social media, open now; hide
  unnamed businesses.
- **About** — version and data sources.

## Without internet

The app shows a notice and keeps what it already loaded; searches resume when the connection
comes back.
