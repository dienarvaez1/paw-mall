# Adoption, rescue and animal protection: reviewed list (2026-10-14)

The DENUE places the Phase 22 rules put under **Adopción y rescate** (111), reviewed by hand on
2026-10-14 (Claude). Owner: mark any that shouldn't be there; the rules or an exclusion will take
them out. Source: INEGI, DENUE (edition of May 2026).

Left out on review (the rules now skip them): 8 vets named "El Refugio" or "El Santuario", and two
municipal control centers ("Centro de Rescate Animal y Control de Zonosis", Delicias; "Unidad de
Control y Protección Animal", Corregidora). Municipal _protection_ units ("Unidad de Protección
Animal", "Dirección de Protección Animal") are in; whether to keep them is part of the pending
decision on control centers.
